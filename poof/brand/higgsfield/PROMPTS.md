# Puff on Higgsfield (Seedream 4.5)

High-resolution Puff stills, made in the Higgsfield web app with the same
setup used for other stills.

## Settings

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Model | Seedream 4.5 |
| Quality | 4K |
| Aspect | 1:1 (mascot stills), 9:16 for the story poster |
| Count | 1/4 (pick the best of four) |
| Unlimited | on |
| Image 1 | `puff-character-sheet.png` (this folder) |

Paste one prompt per run. Every prompt repeats the full description of
Puff on purpose: the model drifts if the traits are not restated.

Check each result before keeping it: the outline is one even dark navy
line, the trail has exactly three puffs, there are no arms, legs, nose,
eyebrows or text. Save keepers to `poof/brand/puff/ai/<mood>.png`.

---

## The base (shared by every mood)

Each prompt below is this base with the mood line filled in.

> Square 1:1 frame. Polished flat vector mascot illustration, smooth and
> crisp like a premium app icon: clean even outlines, flat colours, one soft
> lavender shade along the bottom only. Image 1 is a character sheet; draw
> the cloud character from Image 1, exactly as in Image 1, once: a white
> cloud with one large rounded puff on top, one smaller puff on each side
> and a flat rounded base, all joined in one smooth silhouette with a bold,
> even dark-navy outline; a soft lavender shade across the bottom of the
> base; and a trail of exactly three lavender circles, large to small,
> drifting off from the lower right side. **[MOOD LINE]** Centred, the
> character fills about two thirds of the frame, generous even padding.
> Plain pure white background. Do not copy the sheet's grid or its other
> poses. Exactly one character. No arms, no legs, no nose, no eyebrows
> unless stated. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.

## Mood lines

1. **happy**: Face: two tall rounded dark-navy pill-shaped eyes side by side
   and a small curved closed smile below them.
2. **wink**: Face: the left eye is a tall rounded dark-navy pill, the right
   eye is a closed upward arc (a wink), and a small lopsided smile.
3. **laugh**: Face: both eyes are closed upward arcs, and a wide open
   dark-navy D-shaped laughing mouth.
4. **love**: Face: two bright coral-red heart shapes as eyes and a small
   curved closed smile.
5. **wow**: Face: two round dark-navy eyes each with one small white
   highlight, and a small open oval mouth.
6. **sad**: Face: two dark-navy pill eyes with their outer top corners cut
   at a slant, a small downturned frown, and the three trail circles
   drooping downward instead of drifting up.
7. **sleepy**: Face: both eyes are closed downward arcs and a tiny round open
   mouth; instead of the three circles, three small lavender letter-shaped
   "z" marks rise from the lower right. (Allow these z marks; no other text.)
8. **cool**: Face: one solid dark-navy pair of flat wayfarer sunglasses
   across the eyes and a small sideways smirk.
9. **shy**: Face: two short dark-navy pill eyes glancing slightly down, a
   tiny smile, and two soft coral blush ovals on the cheeks.
10. **party**: Face: both eyes are closed upward arcs and a wide open
    laughing mouth; a small coral party hat with one white stripe, a
    dark-navy outline and a yellow pompom sits tilted on the top puff.
