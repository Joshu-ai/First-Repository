# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Adults sharing everyday moments with close friends and family: quick photos
that disappear once seen, plus light chat and 24-hour stories.

## Product Purpose
Poof is a camera-first messaging web app. Photos open once and are gone;
chats vanish 24 hours after they are read unless kept. Success is people
snapping each other casually and often, without worrying about a permanent
record.

## Positioning
Sharing without a permanent record: a snap is meant to be seen once and then
go "poof".

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Camera, caption and drawing editor, open-once snaps, disappearing chat,
  stories, friends, streaks, personal avatars, live updates.
- Zero-dependency Node server and plain HTML/CSS/JS client.
- Everything visual (mascot, avatars, icon, marketing art) is drawn in code
  as SVG so it stays sharp at any size.
- Clean room: no Snapchat names, ghost, yellow, assets or copy.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Poof (working name; trademark check still to do).
- Mascot: Puff, a cloud. It must read as a mark at icon size (16–48px) and
  also stretch to expressive moments in the app, both equally.
- Puff must not be babyish or kawaii, cluttered, oddly shaped, or generic
  (all four were rejected in the previous design).

## Evidence on Hand
No users, testimonials, press or metrics yet. Marketing must not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Light, not permanent: the product's whole point is that things vanish.
- Adults first: friendly and warm, never childish.
- One clear thing at a time: simple screens, one primary action.
