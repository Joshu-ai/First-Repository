Why "how to write a check" won:
- It's searched a lot: about 190K US searches a month on Google, and it's the #1 Google suggestion for "how to w".
- YouTube autocomplete shows a long tail of extra searches, which the description covers: "to someone", "to yourself", "for rent", "with cents", "for mobile deposit".
- A check is a flat object, so the animation can show every step exactly. A tie or a tire needs real footage.
- The people searching it are new grown-ups (first apartment, first rent check). That's exactly who an "explained like you're 5" channel is for, and it's money-adjacent, which advertisers pay more for.
- "explained like you're 5" is a real YouTube search phrase (it shows up in YouTube autocomplete).

## Script (as voiced, 62.8 s)

1. Here's how to write a check, like you're five.
2. A check is a note to your bank that says: please give this person my money.
3. Spot one, top right: today's date.
4. Spot two: who gets the money. Write their name.
5. Spot three: the amount in numbers. Squeeze it right up against the dollar sign,
6. so this sneaky raccoon can't sneak an extra number in.
7. Spot four: the same amount, in words. Twenty five, and fifty over one hundred.
8. Then draw a line to the end, so nobody can add more.
9. Secret: if the words and the numbers don't match, the bank usually trusts the words.
10. Spot five, the memo: a little note that says what it's for. It's optional.
11. Spot six, the big one: sign your name. No signature, no money.
12. Always use a pen, and never sign a blank check. That's like handing the raccoon your piggy bank.
13. The numbers at the bottom are your bank's name tag and your account number. Leave them alone.
14. Six spots, and you just wrote a check. Which spot didn't you know?
15. Follow for more grown-up stuff, explained like you're five.
16. So next time someone says, just write me a check... *(loops back to line 1)*

Fact check:
- Words beat numbers: UCC section 3-114, "words prevail over numbers" (https://www.law.cornell.edu/ucc/3/3-114). The script says "usually" because a bank can also return a check that doesn't match.
- Routing number = which bank (its "name tag"), then the account number, then the check number. The memo is optional. A check without a signature isn't valid.
- Every name and number on the check is made up: Jamie Lee, Pizza Palace, Piggy Bank & Trust, 123456789.
